CHAPTER I

The Tide Shrine

Chaos has reached Praetoria.

Bound by Planet to protect the sixth mana gate, Djinn Oshannus keeps a watch he never asked to bear.

Now a darkness moves through the waters of an ancient shrine. Its stone guardian, once a protector, has turned against the very gate it was made to defend.

Oshannus follows the troubled tide. He must break the corruption without destroying the guardian—or the shrine beneath its feet.

As he enters the flooded courtyard, cyan light burns within the sentinel’s chest. A stone spear rises from the water.

The reluctant defender raises his hands.
The tide answers.

CHAPTER I COMPLETE

The water remembers

The guardian lowers its spear. Violet light drains from its stone, leaving the shrine intact.

In the settling water, Oshannus sees a trail of corruption flowing toward the broken causeway. This was no isolated disturbance.

He gathers the returning current around his wounds. It eases the pain, but cannot erase it.
